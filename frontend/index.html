Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Material Stewards

Step 1

Upload or Ingest Catalogs

Navigate to Catalog Ingestion. Upload your CPSE Excel/CSV or click "Load 500-Item Industrial Benchmark Dataset". Raw data is archived immutably with SHA-256 hashing.

Step 2

Trigger Cross-CPSE Matching

Go to AI Equivalence Workbench and click "Run Cross-CPSE AI Matching". FAISS KNN indexes semantic vectors and evaluates all attribute pairs for contradictions.

Step 3

Review & Approve Groups

Filter proposed groups by confidence band. Check matched attributes and contradiction alerts. Click "Review Decision" to approve MERGE, MAP, or RETAIN with a technical justification.

Step 4

Verify CNMC & Export ERP Mapping

Inspect generated national records in National CNMC Registry. Head to SAP / ERP Migration Export to download RFC-compliant CSV cross-reference tables.
