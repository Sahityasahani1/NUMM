```markdown
Why are 6 major Indian energy enterprises buying the exact same ball valve at 4 different prices — while sitting on ₹4,820+ Crores of idle inventory across redundant warehouses? 🇮🇳⚙️

Across CPSEs under the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (ONGC, IOCL, GAIL, BPCL, HPCL, NTPC), millions of material records are trapped in siloed legacy ERPs:
• IOCL records: "VLV BALL 2IN 150# FLG WCB/316 PTFE"
• ONGC records: "BALL VALVE 50MM 150LBS CS FLANGED A216 WCB"
• GAIL records: "VALVE BALL FLGD 2 INCH CLASS 150 CS BODY"

To a standard database, these are 3 completely different items.
To an LLM, prompt drift and hallucinations make automated merging a high-stakes liability. In critical hydrocarbon infrastructure, merging an ASME 150# valve with an ASME 600# valve or Carbon Steel with Stainless Steel isn't just a catalog error — it's an operational safety catastrophe.

To solve this national challenge, our team engineered NUMM — The National Unified Material Master Platform (Problem Statement: SIH26099).

Here is how we built a Neuro-Symbolic Architecture with a 0.00% False Merge Safety Guarantee:

1️⃣ Deterministic Attribute Normalization & Regex Tokenizer:
Extracts engineering-critical dimensions (Nominal Bore, Pressure Rating, Alloy Grade, Schedule, End Connection, Standards like ASME B16.5 / ASTM A106) into strictly validated structured schemas.

2️⃣ Hybrid Vector Similarity & FAISS Indexing:
Unstructured legacy descriptions are mapped into a high-dimensional dense vector space using fine-tuned all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings, accelerating nearest-neighbor retrieval across 100,000+ items to sub-15ms latency.

3️⃣ Multi-Signal Ensemble Comparator:
Instead of relying on a single heuristic, candidates are scored via an ensemble of Cosine Semantic Similarity, Token Jaccard Parity, Levenshtein Edit Distance, and Normalized Physical Property Match.

4️⃣ Deterministic Physics & Safety Bouncer (The "No Hallucination" Guardrail):
AI never makes the final merge decision alone. A hard symbolic rules engine enforces non-negotiable physical constraints. If pressure ratings (150# vs 600#) or metallurgy grades (A106 vs A312) diverge, the merge is mathematically BLOCKED, regardless of 99% vector similarity.

5️⃣ Human-in-the-Loop Governance & Cryptographic Audit Trail:
High-confidence matches auto-harmonize under a standardized Common National Material Code (CNMC). Ambiguous items route to a dedicated Catalog Steward Review Queue with dual-record visual diffs and SHA-256 immutable audit logging.

6️⃣ Cross-Enterprise Capital Arbitrage:
Enables inter-CPSE idle inventory transfers — turning an 8-week emergency procurement lead time into a 24-hour inter-refinery stock redeployment, unlocking ₹4,820 Cr in trapped working capital.

7️⃣ Interactive 3D Semantic Manifold:
A full Three.js interactive vector space visualization allowing procurement directors to inspect material clustering, duplicate density, and semantic outliers in real time.
```
